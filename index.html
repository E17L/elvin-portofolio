I'm Elvin Sumitra — a designer and student based in Bali, working at the crossroads of visual identity, digital interfaces, and content that actually stops people mid-scroll.

Design found me through curiosity — starting with math and sciences before landing here. Three years in, I've learned that the best design isn't the most complicated — it's the most intentional.
